This Non-Disclosure Agreement is made between Land Sterling Properties LLC, incorporated and registered in the UAE with Trade Licence 669075, registered office First Floor, Block B, DSO HQ Building, Dubai, UAE (the “Discloser”), and you, the registering recipient (the “Recipient”), in connection with your evaluation of real estate investment opportunities and related confidential information made available through the Land Sterling Investment Portal (the “Purpose”).

1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means all confidential information relating to the Purpose disclosed by the Discloser or its Representatives, directly or indirectly, before, on or after the date of acceptance — including the fact and status of any discussions, the terms of this Agreement, and all proprietary information, opportunity particulars, financial data, analysis, know-how and materials relating to the opportunities and the business of the Discloser and its Group. It excludes information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement, was lawfully held by the Recipient before disclosure, is received from a third party not under a confidentiality obligation, or is independently developed by the Recipient.

2. Confidentiality obligations

The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential; (b) not use or exploit it in any way except for the Purpose; (c) not disclose or make it available to any person except as expressly permitted by this Agreement; (d) not copy or record it except as strictly necessary for the Purpose; and (e) apply adequate security measures to safeguard it from unauthorised access, copying or use.

3. Permitted disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives (its officers, employees and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose) provided it first informs them of its confidential nature and procures that they comply with obligations equivalent to those in clause 2. The Recipient remains liable for the acts and omissions of its Representatives.

4. Mandatory disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the minimum extent required by law, and shall, where lawfully permitted, give the Discloser as much prior notice as possible.

5. Return or destruction

On the Discloser’s written request, the Recipient shall as soon as reasonably practicable return or destroy and erase all Confidential Information, save for copies required to be retained by law or regulation, to which this Agreement shall continue to apply.

6. Reservation of rights

The Discloser reserves all rights in its Confidential Information; no licence or other right is granted beyond those expressly set out here. The Confidential Information is provided without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, does not constitute an offer, invitation or financial advice, and creates no obligation on either party to proceed with any transaction.

7. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone would not be an adequate remedy for breach, and that the Discloser shall be entitled to injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach.

8. Duration

The Recipient’s obligations under this Agreement continue in full force for a period of 5 years from the date of acceptance.

9. Governing law

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Dubai and the UAE, and the courts of Dubai have exclusive jurisdiction.
