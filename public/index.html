About Us

Your trusted partner in making informed property decisions.

Land Sterling is a market leader in real estate consultancy based in UAE, Saudi and UK. Backed with qualified experts with over 100 years of cumulative experience, Land Sterling provides professional property expertise cutting through various regions, including the Middle East, North Africa & Europe. We help our clients with analytical and research-based advice to make informed property decisions, reduce risk and improve overall success. Land Sterling offers a full range of property consultancy services in accordance with RICS and International Valuation Standards across different industries.

In the last 15 years, Land Sterling has accomplished the valuation of 200 billion dirhams worth of properties, has surveyed over 600 freehold buildings including the iconic Burj Khalifa and has served the biggest names in the industry such as Aldar, Emaar, Damac, First Abu Dhabi Bank and other prominent local and international faculties.
